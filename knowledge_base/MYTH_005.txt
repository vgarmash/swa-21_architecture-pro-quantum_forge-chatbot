## As told by the Elders

In the time before counting, when the world was still learning its shape, there occurred the events that explain the gift of the twin moons.

It began when Syloos challenged the boundaries of the known world.

Syloos traveled through valleys where time flowed differently, facing trials that included choices with consequences beyond understanding.

## The Revelation

At the moment of greatest challenge, Syloos realized that true strength comes from community, not isolation.

## The Outcome

From this revelation came the hope that sustains us in difficult times.

## The Moral

This story teaches us that the greatest treasures are often overlooked.
