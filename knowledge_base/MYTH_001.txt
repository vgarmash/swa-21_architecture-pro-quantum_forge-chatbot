# The Legend of The Gift of the Twin Moons

## As told by the Elders

In the time before counting, when the world was still learning its shape, there occurred the events that explain the gift of the twin moons.

It began when The Stone Speaker followed a path of falling stars.

## The Journey

The Stone Speaker traveled through plains where the wind told forgotten stories, facing trials that included riddles posed by ancient guardians.

## The Revelation

At the moment of greatest challenge, The Stone Speaker realized that some truths can only be carried, not owned.

## The Outcome

From this revelation came the wisdom that protects us from folly.

## The Moral

This story teaches us that the simplest truths are the most enduring.

## Transmission

Remember this tale when the seasons change and the world renews itself.
